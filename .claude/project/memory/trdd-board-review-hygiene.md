Fill `implementation-commits:` from `git log --grep=<id>` INTERSECTED with the card's boxes, then drop: commits tagged for ANOTHER card even if the body cites them (consumers, prerequisites), memory-page/docs commits (content, not code — name them in prose next to the box they document), and commits carrying this card's tag but dated after it left `dev` and named by no box (an author still "in" the card tags later work; check the tag's stated version range too). Measured 2026-09-05 on TRDD-SIGBCMGL: a first fill from every backticked SHA in the body put YE15B2JK's and B7DSTJLS's commits and a post-scope CC 2.1.257 pricing entry on the card; two correction commits later the field held two of the original four. Record the correction in the Approval log so a future bisect knows the field was curated, not accumulated.

^ATOM-SD1C-WR66 [desc: "A non-empty blocked-by on a non-blocked column is a defect: move to column blocked with pre-block-column and unblock-when [trdd:<ID8> terminal] (exact grammar; comma-split list; the drift restore also", keywords: blocked-by_set_but_column_ai_review unblock-when_grammar trdd_id_terminal_predicate card_never_auto-unblocked malformed_unblock-when_fails_open pre-block-column drift_detector_restore_blocked_card blocked-by_never_cleared does_the_board_see_archived_cards parse_flow_list_comma_split _PRED_TRDD_RE, type: project, ocd: 2026-09-05, lmd: 2026-09-05]
`trdd-drift.py`: predicate regex `^trdd:([0-9A-Za-z]{8})\s+terminal$` — the bare `trdd:ID` form is MALFORMED and fails OPEN (the card stays blocked forever). `trdd_common.parse_flow_list` splits on commas, so `[trdd:UTFVMVT8 terminal]` survives as one token. `_restore_column_text` rewrites `column:` to `pre-block-column`, bumps `updated:`, sets `blocked-by: []` and deletes the `pre-block-column:` line, so the card cannot re-block itself; per its pass-1 comment and `DESIGN_FOLDERS` loop the uid→column board covers tasks/archived/proposals/refused, so a blocker archived on completion still satisfies the predicate. Before clearing a `blocked-by` instead of honouring it, read the commit that added it (`git show -s --format=%B`): on 2R36W8Q1 the body proved the wait was deliberate. Verified 2026-09-05 (TRDD-2R36W8Q1).


^ATOM-Z5UP-KYAV [desc: "The review step must never silently narrow the card it approves: every strike, split or delegation of a box is logged in the Approval log with who decided, why, and how to veto; and a worker's APPROVE", keywords: review_narrowed_the_card_silently Approval_log_who_decided worker_APPROVE_is_a_claim decide_on_facts_board_verdict orchestrator_re-checked_boxes_first-hand vetoable_reviewer_decision strike_a_box_log_it verdict_is_the_session's_not_the_worker's human_review_reader_judge_the_original_boxes AI_review_verdict_provenance, type: project, ocd: 2026-09-05, lmd: 2026-09-05]
Two invariants that the three open-box cases share. First: the review step never narrows a card silently — a struck, split or delegated box gets an Approval-log line naming the ORCHESTRATOR as the decider, the grounds, and the veto path (owner or USER restores the box, which re-opens the card), and tells the human reviewer which boxes to judge. Second: a lean-worker's APPROVE is a claim, not the session's verdict; before committing it the session re-checks the load-bearing boxes itself (the cited lines, the cited commits' diffs, the saved run output) and writes in the Approval log which checks were its own and which remain the worker's. Measured 2026-09-05: adversarial forks caught the orchestrator relaying a worker's claim as its own on three of the first six cards reviewed (9SYRK6SW, 2R36W8Q1, YBJGIYI1), and mis-filling `implementation-commits` twice on one card (SIGBCMGL). ## Notes and lessons learned

[^1]: [id: ATOM-S6G3-614P, status: valid, keywords: "archived_card_refuses_writes approval_log_append_refused correct_archived_TRDD closing_evidence_before_move trddgrep_immutable archived immutable_error trddgrep_edit_refused_on_archived_card approval_log_exempt_from_freeze review_closing_evidence_before_move_to_complete correction_for_a_completed_TRDD", ocd: 2026-10-17, lmd: 2026-10-17] DO NOT plan to correct an archived TRDD after `trddgrep move <id> complete`, not even through its Approval log, BECAUSE trddgrep refuses every write to an archived card (observed 2026-10-17 on CS2W7E77: an `edit` and an Approval-log `append` were both refused as "archived, immutable", although rule 12 of trdd-design-tasks names the Approval log as exempt from the freeze). DO review and finalize the closing evidence BEFORE the move (stop a closing worker at the move step until its review returns), and if a correction is still needed, put it on a live card that names the archived id.
